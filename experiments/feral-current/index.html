Feral Current gathers six months of playing with sound into six tracks, drawing on 809 distinct sounds from a 1,000-sound pool in Listening Garden. This sedimentation of practice takes new form through algorithmic composition with an AI collaborator. I guided the exploration through artistic direction and listening; together we developed rules and scores that the Garden’s audio engine performed. Garden-processed sounds meet in rhythms that dissolve and return, bringing accumulated experiments into unexpected relationships.

02 / AROUND THE GROOVE

A rhythm to hold onto

There is a lot to enter here. I hear months of Listening Garden activity moving around me; the groove gives that flood a spatial anchor. It stays legible as the garden grows dense around it.

The scores move fragments through space, with grains and echoes that cross and return. The rhythm gives the ear a place to stand as those sounds go around it.

03 / INSIDE THE FIELD

A place to stand

In Floor Takes Root, pulse and bass share a narrow direction. These two passages move from that rhythmic anchor into a field of grains.

TWO LISTENING PASSAGES

The circle at the front marks the pulse and bass anchor. Filled marks show active score events. Each grain has two points, one for each side of its stereo pair. The four colors identify compositional roles; each role can draw on different sounds.

Reading the field

This is a reconstruction of encoder directions from the saved score and the renderer’s motion curves, synchronized to the existing release recording. The native render used one third-order ambisonic field, decoded for stereo listening.

Four selected roles are shown. The view does not measure sound energy or where you will perceive a sound; other roles and effect tails are not drawn. A line between a grain’s two points shows its stereo width. The camera changes only the view, not the audio.
