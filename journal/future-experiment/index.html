NOTES FROM THE GARDEN / 02

An Experiment in
Radical Listening

Letting listening and reflection shape the rules of composition.

BEGINNING SOON / LISTENINGERIC SOUTHER

In this entry

  2. 02 / A listening record
  3. 03 / The question

I am starting another garden soon and staying with it for years. Its source sounds come from my synthesizer and modular practice. I record them, transform them, compose with them, and keep returning to what I hear. The older garden remains a history beside this new one.

01 / THE MATERIAL

Make the sound, then meet it again

Every source sound in this experiment begins with my instruments. I follow what happens as a sound moves through different treatments and musical roles. Is it still recognizable? Does it suggest a new direction when it meets other sounds?

I work in an editable, multitrack space that keeps a sound’s origin connected to its later uses. Each return is a chance to hear it differently and let an unexpected relationship change the direction of a piece.

02 / A LISTENING RECORD

Let the words stay close to the sound

As I listen, I speak annotations tied to the moments I am hearing. The microphone stays on after the piece ends so I can record a separate reflection while the listening is still present. I keep the first reaction, the later thought, and the sound linked, with room for uncertain or changed opinions.

My working vocabulary includes Moss, Bloom, Roots and Vine, and grows from what I actually say. I pay attention to the difference between what I hoped a sound would do and what I hear it doing.

03 / THE QUESTION

Can practice improve the composer?

The goal is to compose work fully algorithmically or with open weight models. My recordings, annotations, and reflections give me a way to follow how the music develops and refine the compositional system through practice.

Over one to three years, or longer, I return to the same questions: how does sustained making and listening change the Garden’s compositions? Can the Garden remember a preference while leaving room for surprise? How does my own taste change as I live with these sounds?
