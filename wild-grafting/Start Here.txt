WILD GRAFTING — LISTENING GARDEN

ON A PHONE
Open the interactive booklet in your browser:
https://listening.garden/wild-grafting/

The web edition includes listening examples, moving ambisonic fields,
six short paired comparisons, and the complete Garden Voices bonus album.
Tap Play to begin. No installation is needed.

TO READ
Open Wild_Grafting_Listening_Booklet.pdf, beside this file.
Its listening links and final-page QR code open the web booklet.

ON A COMPUTER
Unzip the whole folder, then open Open Wild Grafting.html.
Keep the assets folder beside it. The downloaded edition includes the
listening examples, paired excerpts and complete Garden Voices bonus
album for offline use. The full Wild Grafting album is on Bandcamp:
https://listeninggarden.bandcamp.com/album/wild-grafting

TWO CASTS
The paired comparisons appear before the closing "Carry the connections"
section. Choose a passage, then switch between Wild Grafting and Garden
Voices at the same moment. Garden Voices uses an earlier version of the
score; some passages changed before the Wild Grafting release.

FOLLOW THE FIELD
Small field drawings accompany the recordings. The larger Stutter Botany
field lets you turn the view, follow individual roles, or choose a moment
with the slider directly beneath the sphere. The sixteen snapshots show
how the arrangement moves through the passage.

Under "Beneath the leaves: shape a path," sketch a possible spatial path
for a gesture. This is an editable drawing; it does not change the recording.

Headphone listening recommended.
