WILD GRAFTING — LISTENING GARDEN

ON A PHONE
Open the interactive booklet in your browser:
https://ericsouther-source.github.io/listening-garden-booklets/wild-grafting/

The web edition includes the sound examples, moving ambisonic field and
complete Garden Voices album. Tap Play to begin. No installation is needed.

TO READ
Open Wild_Grafting_Listening_Booklet.pdf, beside this file.
Its listening links and final-page QR code open the web booklet.

ON A COMPUTER
Unzip the whole folder, then open Open Wild Grafting.html.
Keep the assets folder beside it. The downloaded edition includes all
listening examples and the complete Garden Voices album for offline use.

Wild Grafting with the Garden Voices appears before the closing
“Carry the connections” section. Choose a track or play all six in order.

The moving field follows a short passage from Stutter Botany.
Turn the sphere or tap a snapshot to explore the arrangement.
These controls change the drawing; the music keeps its original mix.

Headphone listening recommended.
